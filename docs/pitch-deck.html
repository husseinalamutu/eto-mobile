THE PROBLEM

The Ground Reality in the Sahel

📡 The Sahelian Information Paradox
Verified civic grants, agricultural input subsidies, and legal aid exist, but rural citizens remain cut off due to zero internet, bureaucratic opacity, and fragmented announcements.
🔥 Escalation of Resource Disputes
Tensions over communal boreholes and pastoralist grazing corridors frequently explode into violence because frontline monitors lack offline early-warning tools.
⚠️ Armed Roadblocks & Bandit Stops
Field monitors carrying phones through illegal roadblocks mounted by bandits, unknown armed men, or insurgents risk execution or abduction if sensitive incident logs are discovered.
📱 Severe Hardware Poverty
Monitors use 1GB RAM Android Go devices with intermittent power and aggressive OS battery killers that terminate background sync daemons.

THE SOLUTION

Four Pillars of the Eto Architecture

🛡️ Checkpoint Decoy & Panic Wipe
PIN 1234 opens Eto. Duress PIN 9999 launches an authentic Sahel AgriWeather & Grain Market Monitor. Panic PIN 0000 purges the SQLite database instantly.
📋 Offline Opportunity Engine
100% offline database matching verified development initiatives with exact physical desks, required slips, and step-by-step claiming protocols in English & Hausa.
💾 Local SQLite Intake Ledger
Zero-PII intake for resource conflicts and diverted aid. Automatically generates isolated cryptographic UUIDs and timestamps immune to OS cache purges.
⚡ Explicit Manual Sync & Sneaker-Net
Zero background battery drain. Batches records on explicit user action when 2G signal is present, or exports encrypted payloads to SD/USB for physical courier transport.
